About EchoEcho2D

EchoEcho2D is a unique space survival game where you control a spaceship that emits sound waves to reveal invisible enemies. Navigate through the void, sending out pulses to detect and disorient the mysterious astronauts trying to capture you.

The longer you survive, the higher your score - but beware, as your score increases, so does the speed and aggression of your pursuers!

How To Play

Move

Use WASD or arrow keys to navigate your ship through space.

Emit Waves

Press SPACE to send out pulse waves that reveal invisible enemies.

Survive

Stay alive as long as possible! Enemies get faster as your score increases.
